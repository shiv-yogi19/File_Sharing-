    Privacy First

    • Files are transferred peer-to-peer.
    • Files are not permanently stored on FluxDrop servers.
    • Signaling servers do not receive file contents.
    • Temporary identity expires after 48 hours.
    • Room information is temporary.
    • WebRTC connections are encrypted.
    • Application-level encryption is used.
    • Encryption keys are not stored on the server.
